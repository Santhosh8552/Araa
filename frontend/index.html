FAQ

answered.

Clear expectations about AI engines, broker connectivity, risk controls and capital separation.

What makes ARAA Quant AI v4.0 different?

Instead of relying on a single external LLM prompt, AraaBot connects 9 internal engines—including deep technical indicators, GRU forecasting, 3-Agent AI Committee consensus, Quantum market regime detection, walk-forward training and deterministic fallback—so market answers stay grounded in computed data.

Does AraaBot guarantee profits?

No. Trading involves significant financial risk and losses are possible. AI analysis, technical scores and automated bots do not guarantee performance.

How are Paper Simulation and Real Broker funds separated?

Paper mode runs purely on simulated capital inside the terminal. Live execution requires explicit broker credentials and adapter confirmation—with the Company Prop Desk enforcing strict isolation between India INR (Zerodha Kite v3) and Crypto Spot-Only 1x (CoinDCX, withdrawals disabled).

How does the Risk Guardian protect open accounts?

Every order passes independent pre-trade checks inside the 16-state OMS: missing stop-loss protection is rejected, daily drawdown circuit breakers pause new entries when breached, and reconciliation mismatches lock trading until verified.

What happens after I select a plan?

You are guided through account setup and license activation, after which your terminal opens immediately. Proprietary desk operators can also access the separate Company Prop Desk workspace via /company.
